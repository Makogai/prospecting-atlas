Game update: Desert (part 1)
Warning: this guide is subject to change without warning. If you’re using a particular build, consider making a screenshot of it for your reference.
Please use these guides as a starting point. Don’t be afraid to experiment! Your playstyle is unique to you, so what works for someone else might not be ideal for you.
The game stage classifications are meant as a rough guide. Feel free to adapt whichever build suits your fancy, even if you don’t consider yourself to be in that game stage.
Stage V. - Desert: Highest location is Sunscorched Desert.
Stage IV. - Meteor Valley: Highest location is Meteor Valley.
IV.A: No Ascended equipment
IV.B: Have both Ring of Champions and Ring of the Stars 
Stage III. - Swamp: Highest location is Timelocked Sanctuary.
Stage II. - Overgrown Caves: Highest location is Enchanted Ruins/Abyssal Depths.
Stage I. - Snowy Isle: Highest location is Frozen Peak.
Stage 0 - Caldera: Highest location is Infernal Heart.
Bonus Builds: these builds are not really part of the game progression, but you might find them useful (or fun).
A note about the torturer Dredge Master: the Dredge Master’s Ring blueprint can theoretically be obtained by players of almost any level, and the materials to craft it can be found without access to the Swamp. The build guides nevertheless only list it for stage III and up. If you have a DMR, feel free to substitute it for an Umbrite Ring, Vortex Ring, Ring of Thorns, or Mythril Ring.
You might hear about “Sandshaking technique”, as in, “use sandshaking technique for maximum efficiency”. This is a playstyle that tries to minimize the amount of time you spend switching between digging and shaking. It involves arcane magic standing very near the shore and timing your clicking with non-human precision.
You might also hear about “sandshaking tech”. Despite appearances, this is not some miraculous artifact you can equip or buy (unfortunately for those of us who possess neither arcane magic nor non-human precision): it’s just a mistake from people who don’t know that “tech” is short for “technology”, not for “technique”.  
Stage V. Builds - Desert
Highest location is Sunscorched Desert
At this game stage, all equipment is assumed to be six-star and at least 95% quality. All equipment should have the Prismatic mutation.
Five rune slots are assumed, period. (Really. If you can't manage five rune slots, you definitely can't manage 1-in-100-billion-odds ores.)
You must have Ring of Champions ("RoC") and Ring of the Stars ("RoS") for most of these builds, and Dredge Master’s Ring ("DMR") is HIGHLY recommended.


Builds in this stage:
* Luck Efficiency V.
* Hybrid V.
* Summer Hybrid V.
* Size Boost V.
* Items Farming V.


Luck Efficiency V.
Highest average rerolls.
* If you do NOT have a Prismatic RoC, get that first, as it is a requirement for this build. RoS & DMR are also HIGHLY recommended! 
* This is a one-tap build. If you’re not filling your pan with one dig, the efficiency drastically decreases: adjust your equipment, or change some ores in your museum to Mutated LDM.
   * To see if your build 1-taps, use the formula below (while in totems):
Dig Strength × 1.5 >= Capacity
* This build is designed to be used with Luck, Strength, and Luminant totems. You can also use a Friendship totem, but in that case, do not equip the Solitude rune.
* ‡ Treasured isn’t possible for the marked ores. For those, use Mutated.
* ⚠ Not suitable for Fungal Marsh without modification (see notes).

Hybrid V.
Combination of high efficiency, size boost, and modifier boost. 
* This build requires all three of RoC, RoS, and DMR. If you do not have one of them, refer to the Stage IV. builds instead.
* This is a 1-tap build. If you need more than 1 dig to fill the pan, the efficiency drastically decreases.
* Conversely, if you have too much dig strength (Dig Strength × 1.5 >= Capacity), change some museum ores to  Perfect LCW (or Irradiated M).
* This build is designed to be used with Luck, Strength, and Luminant totems, as well as Solar Magnifiers. You can also use Friendship totems, but in that case, do not equip the Solitude rune.
* ⚠ Not suitable for Fungal Marsh without modification (see notes).
* Optimal size boost is 1700+. Optimal modifier boost is 3000+.

Size Boost
Highest possible size boost; †limited equipment (optional)
* ⚠ Not suitable for Fungal Marsh without modification (see notes).
* For best results, use Luck, Strength, and Luminant totems, as well as Solar Magnifiers. You can also use Friendship totems, but in that case, do not equip the Solitude rune.
* ♠If you have the Toughened Candy Cane shovel, use it to trigger the 🍪 XP Cookie effect for an extra size boost. Otherwise/in addition, you can use the consumable item to trigger the effect. (Do not use the Gingerbread pan, though, because it doesn’t have a size boost passive ability.)
* If your Bloodstone in the Exotic slot has a Perfect LCW or Crystalline W modifier, it matches Solarium without these modifiers. However, if Solarium has any of the modifiers listed, it is better.
* Buy Stardust Shake potion from Meteor Valley to increase your Size Boost even further.

Sell Boost V. – SELLING ONLY
Highest possible sell boost; †Optional limited equipment; ⚠ Not suitable for Fungal Marsh
* Equip this build right before selling a full backpack’s worth of ores.
* For best results, use a size boost build to collect the ores.
* If your equipment is not 100%, at least make sure the sell boost stat is maxed out – see the Notes column (these are maximum values for the Prismatic version of the item.) 
* Buy Merchant's Potion from the Shard Merchant to further increase your sell boost.
* If you have the Gingerbread pan and/or the Candy Cane shovel, you can optionally use them to trigger the 🍪 XP Cookie effect for an extra sell boost. Otherwise/in addition, you can use the consumable item to trigger the effect.
* Blank museum slots can be filled with anything you want, or just leave them empty.

At this game stage, strive to make all equipment five-star and at least 75% quality. 
This game stage introduces two important mechanics: the Magma Furnace Forge, which can be used to merge two five-star items into a single six-star item with better stats; and the all-important Museum, which can give you significant multipliers to all of your stats.

* At this stage, the Museum portions of the guides are a suggestion, not a requirement. Do the best you can, even if you don’t have some of the ores, or the ones you do have don’t meet the minimum weight or have the right modifiers.

* You can check what all the ores/modifiers do and the minimum weight requirements on the  museum page in the wiki.

Treasure Chests
Builds and tips related to treasure maps and treasure chests
Applies to Geode farming as well
What are the benefits of opening Treasure Chests?
   * It’s the easiest way to obtain lots of different relics and totems!
   * Treasure Chests are the only source of ores with the Treasured 2L modifier, which is the best choice for Luck Efficiency builds.
   * See https://prospecting.miraheze.org/wiki/Treasure_Chest for a detailed loot pool.
How do I get Treasure Maps?
Each dig has a chance to drop a Treasure Map. In order to obtain Treasure Maps effectively, you need a dedicated build which meets specific criteria:
   * Dig speed = 10%: most important part of the build, achieved by equipping Solar Rings or/and Venomshank (the lower the quality, the better);
   * Treasure Hunter Shovel enchant: grants 2× chance of getting a Treasure Map each dig, and raises map capacity to 10;
   * Excalibur: a charm that introduces a unique statistic - Treasure Map Chance, self explanatory.
Other Tips
   * Quality of digs doesn’t matter.
   * The chance of getting a specific Treasure Map is increased when digging in the same location.
   * You can swap your pans instead of manually emptying your pan, especially if you run a full Capacity build.
   * Use the Walkspeed Build to hunt down chests faster.
   * Use a Size Boost build when opening Treasure Chests to maximize profits - it is the only statistic that affects them. (note: Solar Magnifier and Volcanic Eruption don’t increase size of ores from Chests)
   * Most of the above applies to Geodes as well! The only differences are:
   * Shovel Enchant: Geothermal.
   * Excalibur is not needed.
   * Obviously, you don’t need to collect chests (you can 100% AFK through the process).
   * Luck does affect the rarity of minerals you drop, so you should use the Geode Opening build instead of a Size Boost build to open geodes.
Builds in this category are just examples and suitable for Auto Pan. You can make your own!

   * Get the Infernal enchant on the Dragonflame Pan.

Gathering minerals part 1: Disimproving your Phoenix Heart
Note: if you already have a Phoenix Heart that has a -60% to -70% size boost, you can skip this part. Make it Prismatic (so the size is between -96% and -112%) and move on to “Gathering minerals part 2.”
   * Get 3 Uranium, 2 Starshine, and 1 Inferlume. Go to a crafting anvil and check if they’re small enough to make a one-star Phoenix Heart. If yes, go to the next step; otherwise, keep digging.
   * Craft your new-and-negatively-improved Phoenix Heart. Reforge if needed: aim for the largest-possible negative size boost, -70%.
   * If you own the Crafter’s Luck gamepass and/or have Crafting Mastery, don’t reforge too many times. Craft a new Heart instead.
   * Switch to your usual Luck Efficiency build (including the museum) and collect enough Inferlumes to get the Prismatic mutation on your Phoenix Heart.
   * Best locations for Inferlume: Infernal Heart > Windswept Beach > Sunset Beach > Volcanic Sands
Gathering minerals part 2: Venomshank and Solar Ring, it’s your turn
Note: if you manage to craft a -80% Dig Speed (mutation included) or more Venomshank and own The Excavator Shovel, you can skip crafting Solar Ring at all.
   * Equip your one-star Prismatic Phoenix Heart, and fill the rest of your equipment slots with equipment that gives no size boost.
   * For example, Charm: Excalibur/Witch Hat; Rings: 4×/2× Vortex Ring, 2× Mythril Ring, 2× Purifying Ring
   * Equip the Infernal Dragonflame Pan.
   * Equip any shovel that fits your build (Venomspade, Cupid’s Arrow, Abyssal Shovel, etc.).
   * Load a Full Size Boost Museum.
   * Collect these ores with the lowest weight possible: 1 Bloodstone, 3 Star Garnet, 3 Mythril, 10 Bismuth, 1 Vortessence, 1 Volcanic Core, 2 Fire Opal, 10 Pyronium.
   * Craft a 1-star Venomshank and a 1-star Solar Ring with the lowest Dig Speed possible. (Venomshank: -60%; Solar Ring: -30%.)
   * Again, if you own the Crafter’s Luck gamepass and/or have Crafting Mastery, don’t reforge too many times. Craft a new item instead.
   * Additionally if you use both venomshank and 1 solar ring, you should be able to use a -57% and like -27% - don’t be stressed about getting the very minimum dig speed.
   * Switch to your usual Luck Efficiency build (including the museum) and collect enough Bloodstones and Volcanic Cores to get a Prismatic mutation on your Venomshank and Solar Ring.
   * Bloodstone: Rotwood Swamp > Timelocked Sanctuary.
